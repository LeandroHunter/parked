# Parked

**Never lose your car again.**

Tap once when you park. When you come back, an arrow points the way and shows how far it is.

- One tap to save your spot
- An arrow that leads you back
- Add a note or a photo for big car parks
- Private: everything stays on your phone
- English and German

### [Open Parked](https://leandrohunter.github.io/parked/)

On iPhone, open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
